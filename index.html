Mission

Earth observation AI should belong to everyone — to the African university student writing their first line of geospatial Python, to the government analyst tracking deforestation, to the NGO proving its impact, and to the researcher who can’t afford a per-seat licence for proprietary software.

As founder and sole engineer, I designed and built EOCoreINT as a complete, production-ready digital headquarters — a learning academy and consulting practice sharing the same accounts, payment rails, and codebase. Every course maps to real EOCoreINT curriculum using genuine West African examples: White Volta vegetation, cocoa farms, Lake Volta shorelines, and illegal mining in the Atewa range.

What I Built — End to End
Full course academy — Category → Course → Module → Lesson with video, quizzes, progress, and certificates
B2B consulting engine — quote → project → delivery → invoice with branded PDF generation
Organisation workspaces with role-scoped access for government, NGO, and enterprise clients
Stripe Checkout — one-off course fees, recurring subscriptions, coupons, refunds, webhooks
